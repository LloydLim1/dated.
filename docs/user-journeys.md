# Frontend journeys

The local pilot has six main destinations: For you, Explore (with map discovery), Saved places, My outings, Community, and My journal. Mobile navigation exposes all six destinations. Account actions use the same server-backed account on every screen.

| Journey | Entry and outcome | Recovery and privacy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discover a place | Search or choose a category, city, budget, or occasion. Switch between the result grid and map, then open a place. | Active filters can be removed individually or cleared. Closing the filter dialog cancels unapplied changes. Empty results offer a reset. Place URLs reopen on refresh and follow Back/Forward. |
| Join or sign in | Start from Join Dated or an action that needs an account. After authentication, return to the save chooser, review form, outing form, invitations, or journal you started. | The pilot terms and privacy notice open inside registration without erasing form entries. Password visibility can be toggled. Loading account data does not trigger a mistaken guest sign-in prompt. |
| Save and organize | Save a place into one or more private collections. Create a collection in the save chooser to create it and save the selected place together. | Rename or delete a selected collection. Removing one collection does not remove copies saved elsewhere. Confirmation precedes deletion. Filtering saved places distinguishes no matches from an empty collection. |
| Plan and revise | Start an outing from My outings or a place. Add and reorder up to ten stops, choose a date and visibility, see the total estimated spending, and invite registered companions. | A place can also be added to an existing owned plan. Editing preserves invitations and each participant’s consent. Unsaved form changes have Keep editing and Discard choices. Future outings cannot be marked visited. |
| Design and share an itinerary | Open Dated Premium or an owned outing's Design & share action. Choose a layout/palette, edit the title/introduction, stop order, times, durations, notes and optional planned budgets. Save the design, print/PDF, copy text or download scheduled calendar stops. | Premium is a free preview. A guest signs in and creates an outing first. Only the host can publish/update a shared snapshot. Private notes and companions are excluded. Draft saves do not modify a published copy. Turn off a link to revoke access; publishing again creates a new link. Localhost links require deployment to work on friends' devices. |
| Accept an invitation | Open notifications, inspect the host, date, notes, and ordered stops. Choose visibility separately for each invitation, then accept or decline. | Acceptance defaults to a private association. Accepted invitations lead to My outings. Visibility can be changed, and leaving withdraws the association. The host cannot accept on another person’s behalf. |
| Keep a visited day | Mark an outing visited to move into Visited. Write or edit a review for each stop with the outing’s date already filled in. | Reopen a plan when needed. Deleting a plan preserves standalone reviews. Reviews continue to enforce valid visit dates and category-specific ratings. |
| Share an experience | Write category-specific ratings, visit date, spending, title, story, photos, visibility, and incentive disclosure. Private reviews appear in My journal. | Incomplete ratings and invalid fields prevent submission. Receipt evidence remains separate from public photos. Edit, delete, report, and appeal flows remain available. Closing an unfinished form requires a choice. |
| Find people | Read Discover or Following in Community, open contributor profiles, follow people, vote helpful, or add a comment. | Following access, review visibility, reports, and author ownership are enforced on the server. Community is directly reachable on mobile. |
| Manage an account | Edit name, biography, and city from My journal; export account data; sign out; or delete with password confirmation. | Errors appear in the relevant form or a status message. A branded missing-page screen offers Explore and home links. A route error offers retry and home. |

## Verification

The browser suite covers account authorization, review visibility, private evidence, moderation and appeals, discovery and registration, saving, collection lifecycle, plan edits, history/deep links, unfinished form cancellation, companion consent, mobile invitation acceptance, withdrawal, and mobile Community navigation.

The production screen review captures desktop and mobile discovery, saved places, outings, community, journal, account forms, invitation previews, outing forms, review forms, and their lower scroll regions. Home is also reviewed at laptop and tablet widths. Screenshots and automated accessibility results are in `docs/frontend/`.

## Scope of this frontend

The eight seeded venues and example community stories remain labeled sample content. Map discovery now imports real OpenStreetMap restaurants and cafés; imported place IDs remain stable for collections, outing stops, reviews, and photo posts. Email verification/recovery, hosted infrastructure, public launch, and payments remain outside this local pilot; see `production-plan.md` for those dependencies.

## Map and photo journal journeys

- Open Map discovery → use search, category chips and a collapsible results panel → zoom from prominent city landmarks to neighborhood and street-level places → select a named logo/category symbol or list result to open its page. Labels avoid overlap and controls. Real Dated visits and reviews determine prominence, with mapped landmarks as a fallback; these are not Google popularity rankings. Near me requests location only on click; denied access leaves manual exploration available. Service failures offer Retry this area.
- Open a real place → Post photos here → add 1–10 images, an optional caption, visit date, and audience → publish → see the pictures on the place page and in My journal. No review rating is required. The composer protects unfinished changes and keeps successfully uploaded images when a later upload fails.
- Open My journal → All/Photos/Reviews → select a photo tile → browse by buttons, arrow keys, or swiping → like/comment → edit caption/place/date/audience or remove pictures → delete with an explicit confirmation.
- Public pictures appear in Community and contributor galleries. Following filters posts to followed authors. Private and followers-only posts and their image URLs enforce the same server-side access rules. Unfollowing immediately removes access. Account export includes posts, and deletion and maintenance account for their image attachments.

## Metro Manila discovery and missing-detail journeys

- Filter Museums → choose a museum → read Admission & visiting and its Philippine Museums source → post photos, review exhibits/accessibility/experience/value, save it or add it to an outing. Missing admission does not block a visit review. Museum galleries open photo captions and interactions.
- Museums with known addresses but no confirmed coordinates appear in search and forms with a map-pin-pending notice. They do not appear in nearby searches or as invented map markers. Directory closure notices remain visible while memories stay accessible.
- Menu buttons lead to confirmed Philippine operator menus. Foreign market destinations, unrelated redirects and unconfirmed country sources are suppressed; ratings stay available. Museum pages use admission information rather than a restaurant menu section.

- Select any of the 17 cities/municipalities → the directory filters to that city → Map recenters to its mapped places → zoom to reveal more detailed labels → open a place. City settings include the full Metro Manila list.
- Open a real listing → Menu & prices shows published links and explicit price information, or a clear missing/restricted source message → Write a review remains available → spending can be left blank. Unknown spending and a known zero are stored separately.
- Search by place name, city, or neighborhood in photo, review, and outing forms. Results are limited to a readable set while retaining the selected branch, so the 11,865-place directory does not require scrolling through every option.
- Weekly refresh imports venue changes, verifies logo assets, and checks menu sources. It retains accounts, photos, ratings, collections, and place IDs. If a provider fails or returns incomplete geographic coverage, the last successful directory stays available.
- A place absent from a successful full scan displays a check-before-visiting notice. Its ratings and photo memories remain available; a returning map listing clears the notice. Large unexplained listing-count drops are rejected, and unrelated replacement websites are hidden.
- Verified logos render on place pages/cards/map markers. Missing sources use restaurant, café, museum or cinema symbols; initials are removed. Names appear beside map symbols. Café Guide PH reference links appear only when the source name and city identify one existing listing; ambiguous branches are skipped.
- Filter Cinemas → choose a cinema → see Tickets & showtimes, confirm current availability with the venue, and review or post photos even without published ticket prices. The catalog includes 59 real mapped cinema records; individual screens can be separate mapped records.
- View a location card → see a photograph spanning its cover with an authentic logo/category badge → open the same photo cover on its place page. Public visitor pictures take precedence; category fallback pictures are explicitly illustrative. Private/followers-only pictures never become directory covers.
- Open a place → see the public score, independent reviewer count, supported-visit count and a small-sample notice → expand How the score works → filter experiences to supported or public non-incentivized visits. Each account has equal weight and contributes its latest non-incentivized public visit. Private, hidden, demo and incentivized posts do not affect ratings; support describes assessed evidence rather than verified opinions.
